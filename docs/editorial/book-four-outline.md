# Book Four: Clash in Cleveland

Working outline, October 20, 2026. Book Three's completed manuscript governs continuity; this file carries Book Four's subsequent development. Undrafted chapter details remain proposals unless identified below as accepted direction.

## Chapter map

1. **Something’s Wrong.** Drafted in `content/books/book-four/chapter-01.json`. A loading-platform rescue exposes Seth and Jenny's reduced abilities under pressure. Their teamwork and outside help get the trapped worker clear. Billy and Fist remain unaffected. Chapter 1 is available in the public reader with four selected illustrations and in the current PDF/EPUB exports.
2. **Not Fast Enough.** A crime intervention fails despite sensible teamwork. Civilians are protected, but criminals escape. Public reports begin revealing the team's weakness.
3. **Dork’s Opportunity.** Dork prepares the Army tank and intact shrink/enlarge ray. Jaws eagerly helps. Laura conceals her weakened plasma with excuses. Dork knows nothing of a coming recharge.
4. **An Unspoken Goodbye.** Flaming Head knows returning to the Sun will cost his life. He leaves without telling his friends the trip is one way; he makes no explicit promise to return.
5. **Toward the Sun.** Follow his solitary journey. Keep the actual sacrifice/recharge payoff for Chapter 7 so Laura remains weakened through the vault theft. Chapters 5–6 follow events occurring during the same interval.
6. **The Missing Vault.** Dork shrinks and steals a whole bank vault, then restores it at the workshop. Establish the target's physical separation from the bank before firing. Jaws tries his metal teeth: he gouges accessible locking hardware but cannot break it open. Dork's cutting torch makes slow progress. Laura keeps making excuses when asked to burn it open. They have stolen the vault but cannot yet reach its contents.
7. **What He Left Them.** Flaming Head's sacrifice restores and increases Seth and Jenny's familiar abilities. Give their realization of his absence room before returning to the villains. Laura has not tested her plasma and still believes it weak. Dork, impatient after the failed teeth/torch attempts, insists she open the vault. Her small spit unexpectedly burns through the locking mechanism and into the steel beyond; she is as surprised as anyone. Jaws delights in it. Dork asks why she let them struggle. She hides her surprise behind a claim that she needed to find the right spot. Money becomes accessible here. She does not yet know why her power increased.
8. **Bow to Dork.** Dork brings the tank, ray and Jaws into downtown Cleveland. He demands submission and threatens Terminal Tower. Billy coordinates with police; evacuation and battle geography are established. Specify how Dork operates the tank and uses the ray without inventing a crew.
9. **Lava Lips and Pink Blur.** Laura blocks Jenny's route. Their different responses to accidental harm inform the confrontation. Jenny already knows Laura is Dork's ally and can spit plasma; the increased force is a new danger. Laura's realization of the recharge's source remains to be developed.
10. **Clash at the Tower.** Seth stops the tank while Fist diverts Jaws from civilians. Dork discovers the heroes are stronger and adapts. Jenny is still occupied with Laura.
11. **Before He Fires.** Proposed resolution: Jenny overcomes Laura and interrupts Dork's shot. Coordinated teamwork secures the ray, Dork and Jaws before Terminal Tower is shrunk. Draft the exact restraint and interception mechanics; no accidental cure or restored Luke memories.
12. **For the Land.** Recover the vault and money, account for villain custody, and give the city a complete aftermath. Laura remains powered and accountable. The friends' remembrance preserves Flaming Head's real absence.

## Accepted direction carried into this outline

- Put the vault theft before the recharge aftermath.
- Laura conceals her reduced power through excuses. Dork expects her plasma to open the stolen vault.
- Jaws's teeth and Dork's torch precede the demand that Laura help.
- Laura is pressed into opening the vault in Chapter 7 and is surprised by her increased power. She has not privately discovered it first.
- Chapter 1 begins with the loading-platform rescue and four proposed illustrations.

## Continuity

- Book Three Chapter 14 already establishes Seth and Jenny's weakness. Chapter 1 tests its practical consequences rather than rediscovering it.
- Flaming Head warned Seth/Jenny about Laura; Seth relayed the warning to Billy/Fist. Jenny also inferred that Laura would share their weakening. They have not observed Laura's private test or subsequent excuses.
- Only Seth/Jenny among the rescue team understand Flaming Head's energy communication. Billy/Fist need spoken relays. Fist is one independent floating glove, without an attached body.
- Laura abandoned Flaming Head and remains a villain. No cure or redemption is established.
- The solar sacrifice is real. No hidden survival, ghost or returning spark. Increased strength/speed/plasma do not grant unrelated new abilities.
- Existing Book Four cover remains unchanged, without Laura or Flaming Head.
