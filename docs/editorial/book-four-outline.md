He connects newspaper accounts of the stolen Army ray, missing tank, vanished whole laboratory building and missing vault using red thread on a corkboard. He compares reports, contacts police and starts looking for the relocated building. The last thought is that Orkleson is planning something big. No powers return here.
8. **More Than Before.** Flaming Head's sacrifice restores and increases Seth and Jenny's familiar abilities. Give their realization of his absence room before returning to the villains. Laura has not tested her plasma and still believes it weak. Dork, impatient after the failed teeth/torch attempts, insists she open the vault. Her small spit unexpectedly burns through the locking mechanism and into the steel beyond; she is as surprised as anyone. Jaws delights in it. Dork asks why she let them struggle. She hides her surprise behind a claim that she needed to find the right spot. Money becomes accessible here. She does not yet know why her power increased.
9. **Bow to Dork.** Dork brings the tank, ray and Jaws into downtown Cleveland. He demands submission and threatens Terminal Tower. Billy coordinates with police; evacuation and battle geography are established. Specify how Dork operates the tank and uses the ray without inventing a crew.
10. **Lava Lips and Pink Blur.** Laura blocks Jenny's route. Their different responses to accidental harm inform the confrontation. Jenny already knows Laura is Dork's ally and can spit plasma; the increased force is a new danger. Laura's realization of the recharge's source remains to be developed.
11. **Clash at the Tower.** Seth stops the tank while Fist diverts Jaws from civilians. Dork discovers the heroes are stronger and adapts. Jenny is still occupied with Laura.
12. **Before He Fires.** Proposed resolution: Jenny overcomes Laura and interrupts Dork's shot. Coordinated teamwork secures the ray, Dork and Jaws before Terminal Tower is shrunk. Draft the exact restraint and interception mechanics; no accidental cure or restored Luke memories.
13. **For the Land.** Recover the vault and money, account for villain custody, and give the city a complete aftermath. Laura remains powered and accountable. The friends' remembrance preserves Flaming Head's real absence.
