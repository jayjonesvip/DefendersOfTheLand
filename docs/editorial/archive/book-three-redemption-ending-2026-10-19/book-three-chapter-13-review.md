# Book Three, Chapter Thirteen — Cut the Power

## Manuscript and continuity

The 672-word action chapter (44 passages, six illustrations) continues directly from Laura taking aim in Chapter Twelve. The capture and rescue remain a roughly two-minute event, with only brief necessary dialogue. The aftermath gives Laura room to enjoy an ordinary sneeze.

- Laura uses two small plasma shots to burn the joining/wrist rope and body turns. She frees herself while still powered, before moving toward the collector.
- When Daniel blocks her, she uses a small plasma shot on his loose coat hem to drive him back. He removes the coat and stamps out the flame; he is uninjured and in shirtsleeves afterward. The new coat illustration depicts him in shirtsleeves holding the removed burning coat; it follows ignition and precedes stamping/retreat. The earlier escape image now precedes his attempt to block her.
- Her first lunge and Flaming Head’s “Don’t touch the machine” warning occur here, not in Chapter Twelve. The warning reaches Laura alone inside her mind; Daniel and Luke hear nothing.
- Active contact with the brass rim and upper rail hurts her, locks her grip and draws energy out of her body. This does not imply that collecting already-expelled plasma remotely removes her power.
- Timber chocks prevent a simple roll. Laura’s weight rocks the trolley. Daniel has retreated behind the stairhouse and neither approaches to help nor orders a shutdown.
- Luke tries to stop Laura to protect the machine and his hope of recovering his body. He darts across her path and then clamps his iron jaw onto the control housing to brace against her shove. The housing buckles and the collector explodes; the blast throws him west through the established bricked-up window. Power cuts out as a consequence, not a chosen rescue. The Army ray is elsewhere and untouched.
- The collector goes dark, Laura’s grip releases, glass cracks and weakened Flaming Head escapes through the intake. There is one Flaming Head throughout, never a duplicate in and outside the glass.
- Luke remains unconscious. No memory loss, new identity, delight in his form or loyal behavior is revealed before Chapter Fourteen.
- Laura is visibly ashamed and apologizes for helping trap him. Flaming Head feels no anger toward her: he says he is proud of her choice to help and vows to keep her safe. These final private words arrive while their shared-energy connection fades, before her harmless sneezes. She does not retain ongoing telepathy after her cure. Daniel hears none of Flaming Head’s words.
- Laura’s intact, damp glove and two ordinary sneezes demonstrate her relief. She removes the mask and chooses to go home before Luke awakens. The weakened return to the Defenders is reserved for Chapter Fourteen, before the final villain scene.
