# Book Two finale art reference

Jay Jones approved this cover and asked that its art be used as the reference for the final chapters of Book Two.

Canonical artwork: [book-two-cover-v2.png](../../public/assets/book-two-cover-v2.png). Use this original PNG as an image-generation reference; preserve it. The optimized website copy is `public/assets/book-two-cover-web-v2.webp`. The cover is assigned in `content/books/book-two/book.json` and `print/book-two-cover.json`.

## Character continuity

- **Seth / Super Zerdo:** untidy brown hair, youthful slender athletic proportions, red eye mask, blue full suit with a large red Z, red shoulder accents, gloves, belt and boots. Match his face to the approved cover and established chapter art.
- **Jenny / Pink Blur:** long loose wavy golden-blonde hair and established adult face. For the laboratory capture and revised cover she wears civilian reporter clothes: opaque white long-sleeved blouse, modest pink knee-length skirt, ordinary dark closed-toe shoes, no mask, gloves or superhero boots. She arrives as Jenny, and Daniel and Luke do not know she is Pink Blur. Her superhero uniform remains an opaque pink full suit with continuous white side stripes, white eye mask, gloves and boots for scenes where the manuscript explicitly has her wearing it.
- **Daniel / Doc Dork:** wild white hair, enormous round magnifying glasses, stained white lab coat and dark tie. His triumphant laughing expression belongs to the cover's control-console moment; vary it with the scene.
- **Luke / Jaws O’ Death:** floating spiky blond human head, riveted dark iron bear-trap jaws and pointed metal teeth. No body, neck, hands or limbs. On the cover his anger is directed toward Daniel; future expressions must follow the chapter's action.

## Laboratory and machinery

Use the approved cover for the late-book laboratory's design: crowded analog gauges, switches, thick cables, beakers, dark metal structures and the control console. The large extraction chamber gives off intense orange-gold light, contrasting with deep navy shadows. Cleveland and Terminal Tower are visible through a high window.

Seth and Jenny's separate restraint stations have angled metal backing and broad riveted steel cuffs at their wrists and ankles; Seth wears superhero gloves and boots, while Jenny has bare hands and ordinary civilian shoes. Keep these details consistent when illustrating the planned capture and rescue. Their clothing remains intact. Their faces should show resistance, concern or attempts to escape as appropriate.

The chamber's glow and indistinct silhouette do not establish a new character or explain the transfer mechanism. Follow the eventual manuscript when depicting who or what is inside. Do not introduce an extra captive or a completed transfer merely to reproduce the cover.

## Style and use

Match the cover's detailed hand-inked superhero adventure style: strong dark contours, crosshatched shading, textured colors, blue/pink/red costumes against orange machinery light and navy surroundings. Human faces retain their established identities and modest eye proportions.

For each finale illustration, inspect and supply the original cover alongside any relevant established character or chapter references. Generate a new composition for the actual scene. Chapter illustrations omit the cover title, author lettering and other cover typography. The cover is a visual reference, not a requirement to repeat its pose or composition in every scene.

The finale remains in development: Jenny is trapped to lure Seth, Seth is gassed and restrained, Daniel seeks Flaming Head's energy for Skyfall, Luke hopes to regain a body, and the final chapter contains the rescue. Keep the art aligned with the final authored version of those events.

The original v1 cover is preserved for history. The v2 cover changes Jenny to civilian clothes to match Chapter 16 and the planned continuing captivity. Do not change her into costume during captivity without an explicit authored event.
