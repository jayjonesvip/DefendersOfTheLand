## Character continuity

- **Seth / Super Zerdo:** untidy brown hair, youthful slender athletic proportions, red eye mask, blue full suit with a large red Z, red shoulder accents, gloves, belt and boots. Match his face to the approved cover and established chapter art.
- **Jenny / Pink Blur:** long loose wavy golden-blonde hair, natural green eyes (Book One canon) and established adult face. For the laboratory capture and revised cover she wears civilian reporter clothes: opaque white long-sleeved blouse, modest pink knee-length skirt, ordinary dark closed-toe shoes, no mask, gloves or superhero boots. She arrives as Jenny, and Daniel and Luke do not know she is Pink Blur. Her superhero uniform remains an opaque pink full suit with continuous white side stripes, white eye mask, gloves and boots for scenes where the manuscript explicitly has her wearing it.
- **Daniel / Doc Dork:** wild white hair, enormous round magnifying glasses, stained white lab coat and dark tie. His expression follows the scene; in Chapters 18 and cover v4 he is frustrated and concentrating, not celebrating a successful transfer.
- **Luke / Jaws O’ Death:** floating spiky blond human head, riveted dark iron bear-trap jaws and pointed metal teeth. No body, neck, hands or limbs. On the cover his anger is directed toward Daniel; future expressions must follow the chapter's action.

## Laboratory and machinery

Use the approved cover for character faces, inking and equipment atmosphere, with its spatial layout superseded by the author-approved basement continuity: crowded analog gauges, switches, thick cables, beakers, dark metal structures and the control console. The cover’s orange-gold machinery lighting and navy shadows remain style references. Its tall unobstructed skyline window depicts the earlier layout; it does not establish a basement sightline. Current canon uses a dry hidden workshop beneath the original upstairs laboratory, an ordinary service door opening into a sunken yard, and a small high window above the workshop bench looking into that yard. The collector remains unfinished and empty. Preserve the approved cover file, but flag its geometry for author review before final publication; do not reproduce its city-facing windows in replacement chapter art.

Seth and Jenny's separate restraint stations have angled metal backing and broad riveted steel cuffs at their wrists and ankles; Seth wears superhero gloves and boots, while Jenny has bare hands and ordinary civilian shoes. Keep these details consistent when illustrating the planned capture and rescue. Their clothing remains intact. Their faces should show resistance, concern or attempts to escape as appropriate.

The clear cylindrical collector is visibly empty and unfinished through Chapter 18 and on cover v4. Show disconnected wiring; no silhouette, energy vortex, fireball or glow sourced from inside it. Do not introduce an extra captive or completed transfer.

## Style and use

Match the cover's detailed hand-inked superhero adventure style: strong dark contours, crosshatched shading, textured colors, blue/pink/red costumes against orange machinery light and navy surroundings. Human faces retain their established identities and modest eye proportions.

For each finale illustration, inspect and supply the original cover alongside any relevant established character or chapter references. Generate a new composition for the actual scene. Chapter illustrations omit the cover title, author lettering and other cover typography. The cover is a visual reference, not a requirement to repeat its pose or composition in every scene.

The completed finale preserves these stakes: Jenny is trapped to lure Seth, Seth is gassed and restrained, Daniel seeks Flaming Head's energy for Skyfall, and Luke hopes to regain a body. Chapter 20 contains the rescue; Chapter 21 closes with its reporting and election consequences and the team together. Keep the art aligned with the final authored version of those events.

The original v1 cover is preserved for history. The v2 cover changes Jenny to civilian clothes to match Chapter 16 and the planned continuing captivity. Do not change her into costume during captivity without an explicit authored event.

The v3 cover adds visibly scuffed Fist-O-Mite, a floating red boxing glove with expressive eyes and a torn empty white cuff, inside a reinforced round-topped steel bird cage. No arm or body is attached. Chapter 17 establishes his injury and capture; match those details in later rescue art. Preserve the v1 and v2 cover originals. The collector is still empty at Chapter 17’s ending.
