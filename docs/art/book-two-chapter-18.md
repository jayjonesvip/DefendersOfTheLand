# Book Two, Chapter 18 art beats

Seven illustrations completed and integrated into the manuscript. Original PNGs are preserved in `docs/art/originals/book-two/chapter-18/`; optimized WebP copies are in `public/assets/book-two-chapter-18-*-v1.webp`. Match the established Chapter 17 laboratory and character appearances. Jenny remains in her civilian white blouse, pink skirt and shoes; Seth wears his blue and red uniform. Fist is a red boxing glove with eyes, scuffed and creased leather and a torn empty white cuff, without human fingers.

- Flaming Head at the high window; Daniel beside the unfinished collector. His encouragement reaches Seth and Jenny silently through shared energy, without speech directed at Daniel or Luke.
- Luke pursuing Flaming Head outside while Daniel watches; Fist later intercepts unevenly, still visibly injured.
- Seth concentrating on one wrist cuff, bending it with his red-gloved hand.
- A small plasma ball gathering beneath Jenny’s shoes while her wrists and initially both ankles remain restrained.
- The cage burning open above and away from injured Fist.
- Daniel turning back in disbelief at Jenny’s freed legs and Seth’s bent cuff.
- Closing beat: unconscious Jenny’s legs secured with leather straps; unconscious Seth’s station tips backward into an operating table. Daniel urgently prepares the controls.
